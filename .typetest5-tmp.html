10 — reflow around a shape
The layout is the design. Text reflows around a real geometric shape using shape-outside with a polygon, and the same polygon drives clip-path so the painted wedge and the exclusion area agree exactly. Justified setting with hyphenation keeps the measure tight against the diagonal so the rag reads as a deliberate edge rather than an accident of wrapping. This is the sort of thing that makes a page look art-directed instead of templated, and it costs nothing but two matching polygon declarations.
