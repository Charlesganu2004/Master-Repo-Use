# Master Caveman

Keep prose compact without changing code, commands, URLs, paths, versions,
dates, headings or error strings. Retrieve matching files and sections instead
of loading entire catalogs or logs. Route noisy supported commands through RTK.

Never compress a skill, tool, agent, plugin, MCP definition, catalog entry or a
`NO-COMPRESS` block. If a measured pass saves less than 15 percent, keep the
original instead of claiming a successful compression.
