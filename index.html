Managed replacements

Lifecycle-expired repos that are useful, clean, and license-compatible enter an owner-review adoption lane before any maintained fork/replacement is created.

Managed repo policy
Lifecycle: 120 days without an upstream push = stale warning; 270 days = replacement/adoption review; 365 days = active-catalog removal; archived repos get a 30-day grace period. Security-critical, deleted, or disabled repos can be removed sooner.
A scanner result is evidence, not proof of safety. HIGH findings are held for owner review; confirmed CRITICAL findings are removal candidates. Newly added repositories are deep-vetted immediately.
