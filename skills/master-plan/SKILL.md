# Master Plan

For work with more than a couple of dependent steps, record the objective,
constraints, evidence to inspect, implementation slices and verification gate.
Keep the plan live as discoveries change it. Parallelize independent slices and
name the stopping condition. Do not add ceremony to a one-step task.
