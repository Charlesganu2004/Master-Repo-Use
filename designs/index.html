Atlas designs

Five ways through the same system. They share one data layer, so a lane added anywhere appears in all of them. What differs is how the map is drawn and how you move through it, not what anything means.

All five read 127 lanes · 368 components · 10 hybrid routes · 17 families, generated from files that exist in this repository.

1. Graphite
structured lanes · the one you have

Horizontal lanes with lenses. The original. Reading order runs top to bottom, and lenses dim rather than split, so the whole topology stays on screen while you focus one slice.

Open Graphite
2. Constellation
force-directed · drag to explore

Physics, not layout. Components repel, connections pull, and same-lane parts cluster on their own. Drag any star and the cloud reorganises around it. Selecting one lights its links and fades everything else.

Open Constellation
3. Console
keyboard-first · type to filter

No mouse required. One filter box over all 368 components, arrow keys to move, enter to open. Fastest of the five when you know roughly what you are looking for and want the command out of it.

Open Console
4. Orbital
concentric rings · one ring per family

Depth as distance. Families are rings ordered outward by where they sit in the system. The field rotates slowly; click a ring label to filter, click an orb to draw spokes to everything it touches.

Open Orbital
5. Blueprint
drafting sheet · pan and zoom

A schematic you survey. Every lane is a titled box on a grid, packed to stay square. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Connections route as orthogonal traces with flow animation, the way a wiring diagram shows direction.

Open Blueprint

What every design carries

The differences are visual and navigational. These are identical in all five, by design:

  • Hardware first. A scan command for your platform sits above everything, because what the machine can host gates which routes are real. Enter your RAM and the routes re-verdict.
  • Ten tabs. Map, Agents, Skills, Tools, Plugins, MCP, Hybrid routes, Hardware, Custom lanes, Suggest.
  • Click anything for its description, its lane and that lane's source file, everything it connects to, the hybrid routes it belongs to, and the command to run it on your OS.
  • Lanes intermix. Family and kind filters compose rather than replace.
  • Custom lanes and suggestions are stored in your browser only. Nothing is transmitted from these pages.

These pages fetch atlas-data.json, which browsers block over file://. Serve the folder instead: python -m http.server 8000
